# 🚀 LaunchProof

> Validate your startup idea before you spend months building it.

LaunchProof is a lightweight AI-powered web application that helps aspiring founders evaluate their startup ideas in just a few minutes. Instead of relying on gut feeling, users receive structured feedback on the strengths, weaknesses, market potential, and possible risks of their idea.

Whether you're a student working on your first project, participating in a hackathon, or planning your next startup, LaunchProof gives you a quick reality check before you start building.

---

## 📌 Why LaunchProof?

Many startup ideas fail because people build first and validate later.

LaunchProof was created to solve that problem by helping users answer questions like:

- Is this problem worth solving?
- Who are the target users?
- How competitive is the market?
- What are the biggest risks?
- Is the idea actually feasible?

Instead of random opinions, the application generates an AI-based evaluation that helps users make more informed decisions.

## 💻 How It Works

1. Enter your startup idea.
2. Submit the idea for analysis.
3. The AI evaluates multiple aspects of the concept.
4. Review the generated validation score and recommendations.
5. Improve your idea based on the feedback.
